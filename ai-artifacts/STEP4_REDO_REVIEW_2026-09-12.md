### Pre-empting the likely objection that a 64-bit FNV digest makes the cache unsound [MINOR, REJECT_CANDIDATE, lens None]

**file_line**: /home/qiyuan/Current/MLML/contrib/phi-system/Phi_Logic_Programming_Reasoner/library/guard_cache.ML:77

**claim**: A reviewer will object that the whole cache hangs on a 64-bit FNV-1a digest with no re-verification of the key, so a collision serves one guard's entry to a different guard. I raise it only to close it: it is nitpicking, and no change is warranted. A collision cannot produce a wrong theorem. Every cached lemma is re-certified and re-proved before use -- `replay_lemma` (reasoners.ML:1374-1384) runs `Thm.cterm_of ctxt0 stmt` and then `eval_prf_str` on the recorded method text in ctxt0, and a lemma that fails either step is dropped with a warning. A `Proved` verdict is never trusted either: the Proved arm still requires P-auto to prove the goal in this call (reasoners.ML ~1478), and a Proved entry that no longer proves raises the loud guard warning. The only verdicts consumed without re-derivation are `Refuted` and `Unknown`, and both merely make the reasoner give up on a branch -- they can cost a lost proof search, never a false theorem. Separately, `Hasher.terms` is not structurally ambiguous: each constructor contributes a distinct tag and a fixed-arity walk (Hasher.ML:156-215), so two different term lists cannot yield the same operation sequence; only a genuine 2^-64 collision can, and at a few hundred entries per theory store that is far below every other risk in the system.

**evidence**: Verified in source. reasoners.ML:1374-1384 (replay_lemma re-certifies and re-proves); reasoners.ML ~1478 (the Proved arm re-runs P-auto and warns loudly if it fails); reasoners.ML ~1487-1492 (Refuted and Unknown only end the call with an empty sequence); Hasher.ML:156-215 (tagged, fixed-arity hash walk); Hasher.ML:230 (`terms ts = fold hash_term ts fnv_offset_basis`). Not measured at runtime.

**proposal**: No change. If this objection arrives from another reviewer, reject it with the above: the cache never trusts a hit far enough for a collision to become unsoundness, only far enough for it to cost a branch. Recording the reason once in the plan's §8 (a sentence saying the digest is a lookup key and never an authority, because every consumed hit is either re-proved or merely a give-up) would be enough to stop it recurring, and that sentence is the only thing I would spend effort on.
